# Corevia Academy academic quality and learner-trust standard

## Purpose

This standard governs the quality of every paid Corevia Academy programme. A stage must not be described as academically complete merely because it has a title, a set number of lessons, a quiz, or a completion certificate.

## Programme-level expectations

| Stage | Required learning evidence |
|---|---|
| Certificate | Correct foundational explanations, guided practical performance, safe and ethical conduct, and a short evidence portfolio. |
| Diploma | Independent multi-step application, troubleshooting, technical documentation, quality assurance, and a case-based project. |
| Degree | Substantial disciplinary theory, critical use of frameworks, research literacy, evaluation of alternatives, and an evidence-supported applied project. |
| Masters | Specialist literature synthesis, critical comparison of competing approaches, justified advanced methods, independent design, evaluation, and a substantial capstone. |
| PhD | A defensible research gap, original research question, critical engagement with primary literature, rigorous and ethical methodology, reproducible evidence, limitations, an original contribution, thesis-style report, and oral defence. |

## Mandatory content requirements for every subject and stage

1. **Subject specificity:** lessons, examples, case studies, assignments and assessment questions must test the named subject. Reused generic questions are not sufficient evidence of subject mastery.
2. **Traceable study material:** each programme must provide a reading list or source guide containing real, verifiable books, standards, official guidance or scholarly publications. Do not invent references, authors, DOIs, findings or institutional endorsements.
3. **Learning outcomes:** publish measurable outcomes using verbs appropriate to the stage. Map every lesson, assignment and assessment to one or more outcomes.
4. **Progressive depth:** later stages must add disciplinary complexity, independence, critical reasoning and research depth; they must not merely reword the same introductory lesson.
5. **Assessment validity:** assessments must measure the stated outcomes. Multiple-choice questions may test knowledge but cannot alone establish advanced professional competence, Master's-level synthesis or doctoral originality.
6. **Authentic work:** Diploma and above require applied work. Degree and above require a substantial evidence-supported project. Masters requires a critical capstone. PhD requires original research and a defensible contribution.
7. **Feedback and integrity:** provide explanations or assessor feedback, require appropriate citation, and prohibit plagiarism, fabricated data and misrepresentation of sources.
8. **Learner transparency:** show the syllabus, learning outcomes, workload expectations, assessment method, pass criteria, fee, prerequisite and what the completion certificate does and does not represent before checkout.
9. **Credential accuracy:** Corevia Academy completion certificates record completion of the stated programme. Do not describe them as government-accredited, university-awarded or professionally licensed qualifications unless the relevant approval has actually been obtained.

## Release gate

A subject-stage is ready for paid enrolment only when a qualified subject reviewer has checked and recorded:

- factual accuracy and currency;
- sufficient depth for the advertised stage;
- authentic subject-specific examples and exercises;
- verifiable references and reading guidance;
- alignment between outcomes, lessons, project and assessment;
- accessible instructions, marking rubric and feedback;
- ethical, safety and legal considerations;
- transparent fee and credential wording.

A stage that has not passed this review must remain marked **in development**. Passing a software build or deployment is not an academic quality approval.

## Minimum assessment design

- Certificate: knowledge checks plus a guided practical demonstration.
- Diploma: knowledge checks plus an independently completed applied case and evidence portfolio.
- Degree: analytical assessment plus a substantial applied/research-informed project.
- Masters: critical synthesis, advanced capstone and a defensible evaluation.
- PhD: proposal/gap review, methodology and ethics review, original research evidence, thesis-style submission and oral defence.

The existing 75% quiz threshold can remain a knowledge-check threshold, but it must not be the sole pass condition for Diploma, Degree, Masters or PhD programmes.

## Learner payment confidence

Before payment, learners should be able to inspect the complete syllabus and sample lesson, see the exact price and progression prerequisites, understand the assessment and feedback process, and contact Academy support. Payment confirmation must be separate from academic completion.

### Cross-cutting findings and severity

| Severity | Finding | Evidence observed | Required remediation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Critical | Advanced-stage completion currently relies on a multiple-choice assessment and a 75% threshold. | The assessment API creates the completion record and changes enrolment to COMPLETED when the MCQ score reaches 75%; it does not collect or assess the project/capstone described in the pathway lesson. | Implement and test a persisted practical/project submission and assessor rubric workflow before representing Diploma, Degree, Masters or doctoral research preparation as assessed beyond a knowledge check. |
| High | The pathway generator reuses a common set of eight generic assessment templates for every subject and stage. | The template stems, correct responses and distractors are shared across subjects; subject titles and lesson titles are inserted into the same generic framing. | Replace generic items with reviewed, discipline-specific question banks and map each item to a measurable subject outcome. Use subject expert review for high-stakes content. |
| High | The lesson generator asks learners to find authoritative books, standards and research but does not itself supply a verified course-specific bibliography. | The generated lesson text contains instructions to consult authoritative sources and record bibliographic details; no actual bibliography is emitted by the pathway generator. | Add verified subject-specific reading lists with stable bibliographic details and source checks; do not fabricate references. |
| High | Course-stage depth is expressed mainly through stage-specific wording and project descriptions; independent evidence of distinct syllabi and worked solutions has not been recorded. | Six common unit titles and common lesson structure are generated for all subjects, with base lesson material inserted as source notes. | Review the actual lesson content per subject and stage; add solved examples, worked calculations/demonstrations, discipline-specific cases and progressively harder practical tasks. |
| High | A paid course can be completed without submission of the project described in its lesson material. | The current learner UI presents lessons and a final MCQ assessment; no project upload/submission/review control is present in the inspected Academy page. | Add a transparent assessment breakdown and enforce the project component in the completion state, preserving existing enrolment/payment records. |
| Medium | Learners are told the fee and pathway sequence, but a complete pre-payment view of syllabus, sample lesson, assessment components, feedback service and reviewer status was not established in the inspected catalogue flow. | Catalogue shows fee, stage, description and prerequisites; detailed lesson content is returned only for enrolled learners by the courses API. | Publish a useful syllabus and sample lesson, exact assessment method, workload, feedback turnaround/availability, prerequisite and completion-certificate wording before checkout. |
| Medium | Learner-facing wording calls the last pathway “PhD” in stage labels, although the description correctly says it does not confer a PhD. | Pathway stage label and progression display use “PhD”; description disclaims university award/protected title. | Prefer “Doctoral Research Preparation” as the learner-facing name throughout; retain an explicit statement that no PhD degree is awarded. |

### Subject-specific review prompts

The following are the minimum discipline-specific checks to complete before assigning a stage rating:

- **Agriculture and agribusiness:** crop/livestock systems, local agroecology, farm records, input economics, safety, climate resilience and field-based evidence.
- **AI literacy and responsible AI:** model limitations, data privacy, bias, evaluation, safe tool use, disclosure and realistic task-based verification.
- **Literacy, English and study skills:** age-appropriate progression, comprehension, writing samples, feedback, inclusive language and authentic learner artefacts.
- **Numeracy, mathematics and physics:** worked calculations, units, diagrams, derivations, problem sets, correct solutions and misconception checks.
- **Biology, chemistry and primary science:** accurate scientific explanations, safe practical protocols, observation records, uncertainty and source traceability.
- **Business, accounting and finance:** realistic but clearly labelled case data, reconciliations, financial calculations, controls, ethical decisions and Kenyan context where relevant.
- **Computing, cybersecurity, data and web development:** executable/practical tasks, test cases, security boundaries, data handling, debugging evidence and reproducible outputs.
- **Marketing, e-commerce and freelancing:** campaign/storefront/portfolio artefacts, measurable objectives, analytics, customer protection and platform-policy awareness.
- **Electrical technical skills awareness:** safety-first scope, hazard identification, standards awareness and no instruction implying unqualified learners may perform regulated electrical work.
- **Leadership, entrepreneurship, procurement and project management:** stakeholder cases, budgets, procurement integrity, risk registers, measurable delivery and documented decisions.
- **Social studies and citizenship:** age-appropriate civic concepts, balanced sourcing, local context, respectful discussion and evidence-based interpretation.
- **Research methods and critical thinking:** research question quality, literature search, methods, sampling, ethics, analysis, limitations, reproducibility and defensible claims.

### Release decision

**No course-stage is cleared for a paid academic-quality claim by this initial audit.** This is an evidence-status decision, not a finding that every lesson is factually wrong. The actual 1.3 MB course catalog and each of the 150 generated subject-stage combinations still require readable inspection, course-specific scoring, reference verification and qualified subject review. Software deployment success is not academic validation.

The five-level model must be described as Corevia learning programmes that issue certificates of completion. The final pathway is doctoral research preparation only and must not be represented as a PhD award, university degree or accredited qualification.

# Accounting Software & Digital Bookkeeping Practice — paid pilot release

**Course ID:** `accounting-software-practice-certificate`  
**Fee:** KES 1,500  
**Release mode:** Company-authorised paid pilot  
**Credential:** Non-accredited certificate of completion only

## Release boundary

This pilot exception is deliberately limited to the Accounting Software & Digital Bookkeeping Practice Certificate. It does not mark the programme academically approved, does not create an academic sign-off, and does not mark any candidate reference as verified in the Academy database.

The learner-facing course page must disclose that academic review continues and that the completion certificate is not a university award, professional licence, or accredited qualification. The catalogue must continue to report the academic approval status accurately.

## Payment and enrolment

The existing Paystack payment initialization, exact KES amount, provider-reference validation, payment confirmation, idempotent enrolment activation, and course revenue ledger processing remain in force. Only the course-specific academic checkout gate is bypassed for this pilot course ID.

No other programme stage receives this exception. Other programme stages remain closed until their own required references and sign-off are complete.

- Do not describe the course as accredited or as a professional qualification.
- Keep the published syllabus, price, assessment threshold, and completion conditions visible before checkout.
- Confirm every payment with Paystack server-side before activating access.
